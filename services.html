01Google Business Profile

Most profiles we audit are held back by the wrong category or a half-filled listing, and Google treats them that way. We build yours out fully: the categories that decide which searches you show up in, then services, photos, hours and a booking link. After that we keep it active every week, so the profile looks alive and earns the call.

What we handle

  • Category selection, where most profiles go wrong
  • Full service list, description, hours and attributes
  • Photos and Q&A that answer customers before they ask
  • Booking or appointment link, built for you if needed
  • Weekly Google posts so the profile stays active

Reviews & Reputation

02Reviews and Reputation

What moves rankings is not the total count but a steady flow of recent reviews that mention the service you want to rank for. We set up automated requests after each job, invite customers to mention the service they had, and reply to every review, good or bad, in your name. Then we track how you stack up against your top three local competitors.

What we handle

  • Automated SMS or email review requests after each job
  • Customers invited to mention the service they received
  • Every review answered, positive and negative
  • Replies that reinforce your service and location
  • How many, how often and how recent, tracked against competitors

Citations & NAP

03Citations and NAP

Google cross-checks your details across the web, and in Lithuania that means rekvizitai.lt, visalietuva.lt, 1588.lt, Facebook and the directories your industry uses. One old phone number or a second address on any of them quietly weakens trust in your profile. We audit every listing, standardise the details, and find and remove the duplicates that split your authority.

What we handle

  • Full audit of your details across every platform
  • rekvizitai.lt, visalietuva.lt and 1588.lt corrected and aligned
  • Facebook and industry directories brought into line
  • Duplicate listings found and removed

We skip the 200-directory packages, because a few accurate listings beat hundreds of sloppy ones.

04Website and On-Page

This is the part that cannot be automated well, and it is where durable rankings come from. We build a dedicated page for each service in each location you serve, with the technical markup that tells Google exactly what kind of business you are, and contact details that match your profile to the letter. Fast on mobile, and written in Lithuanian by people, not translated into it.

What we handle

  • One page per service, per location
  • Titles and headings that pair service with city naturally
  • Structured data that names your exact business type
  • Footer details and map matching your profile exactly
  • Internal links that pass authority to service pages
  • Mobile speed and Google page-experience checks handled

05Local Link Building

Google trusts a business the local community trusts. A link from the chamber of commerce, an association you already pay dues to, or the club you sponsor carries more weight than anything bought in bulk. We find and secure these for you, including partner pages with your suppliers, and pitch your story to local news when there is one worth telling. These links come slowly, but they last and are hard for competitors to copy.

What we handle

  • Chamber of commerce and industry association listings
  • Sponsorships of local clubs and events turned into links
  • Supplier and manufacturer partner pages
  • Local news pitches and PR
  • Guest contributions to local blogs

06Engagement Signals

Calls, direction requests, website clicks and photo views tell Google whether your profile is worth showing. Nobody can set these directly, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. We watch them closely because they are the earliest sign that the work in the other six areas is landing.

What we track

  • Calls from your profile
  • Direction requests
  • Website clicks
  • Photo views
  • Trends explained in plain language each month

This is not directly controllable, so we report on it honestly rather than promise numbers.
